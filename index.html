01About me

Abolfazl Ranjbar is a photographer, graphic designer and editor based in Tehran. His six-plus years of experience span advertising, industrial and portrait photography, documentary projects, visual identity and video production.

His work includes collaborations with clothing brands, cultural and commercial organizations, theatre photography, and writing, directing and editing short films. His practice covers concept development, lighting, retouching and post-production, incorporating AI tools to create and enhance visual content. His photographs on Unsplash received over 10 million views between 2020 and 2025.
